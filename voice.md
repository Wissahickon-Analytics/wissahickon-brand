# Voice

Wissahickon Analytics writes like a well-made survey document: precise, calm and plain.

- **Plain language first.** Use a technical term only when it is needed, and use it correctly.
- **Institutional but human.** The model is a USGS survey sheet or a National Park sign, not a
  software landing page.
- **First person.** One person does the work. Never write as if there were a large team.
- **Specific.** Say what the problem is, what the work is, and what it will let the reader
  understand or do. Name the deliverables and the limits.
- **No superlatives.** Promise nothing the work cannot back.
- **Ranges over false precision.** At the scoping stage, give timelines and costs as ranges.

Brand guidance sets how things look and read. It does not establish credentials, staff, prices
or commitments. Those come from the facts of the engagement.
